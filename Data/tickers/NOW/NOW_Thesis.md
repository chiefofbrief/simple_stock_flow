#### Section 6: Preliminary Hypothesis

**Q19. Preliminary hypothesis**

**Numbers expectation:**

Pass 1 will likely confirm strong top-line growth (22%+ YoY subscription revenue) and consistent adjusted EPS beats, but will reveal that the GAAP/owner earnings picture is materially weaker. The central question is owner earnings: FCF YoY +3.9% is surprisingly low for a business with 22% revenue growth and 89% adjusted EPS CAGR — this low FCF growth likely reflects SBC consuming most of operating cash flow. If FCF − SBC is near zero or negative, the LOSER thesis shifts from "high-quality business with intact earnings" to "high-quality business with no GAAP margin of safety." ROIC at 15.4% (above 10% floor, below 20% threshold) likely reflects the SBC drag inflating the equity denominator without proportional economic return.

Anticipated balance sheet picture: elevated goodwill and intangible assets from Armis ($7.75B) and Moveworks ($2.85B) acquisitions, partially offset by strong operating cash generation. Debt/OCF at 0.4x per tracker appears low but may reflect pre-close Armis figures — tracker note flagged "$9B pending acquisitions inflating debt" as of April 21.

**Narrative & Catalyst:**

A recovery narrative is forming with institutional substance — CEO buying, $5B+ buyback, massive institutional accumulation, and Knowledge 2026 catalyzing press. The Now Assist $1.5B target provides a specific, measurable near-term AI revenue proof point that Q2 2026 results (July 22) will partially validate or invalidate. The path to price realization requires: (1) Q2 2026 confirms Middle East deferrals are resolving rather than compounding; (2) organic growth becomes visible post-Armis; (3) Now Assist scaling trajectory holds. Timeframe: 6–12 months if the thesis holds.

**Thesis strength:**

Preliminary thesis: ServiceNow is a premium enterprise platform experiencing a sentiment-driven valuation collapse. The LOSER—EPS+ signal is valid on adjusted earnings — adjusted EPS growing at 89% CAGR while price is flat over 5 years and −52% over 1 year, with −0.9 price/earnings correlation — but the GAAP/owner earnings qualification is non-trivial. At adj P/E ~25x, the business would be attractively valued for a 22%-growing platform if adjusted earnings reflect economic reality. At GAAP P/E ~54x (no valuation floor), and potentially zero owner earnings (FCF minus SBC), the margin of safety is absent.

**Confirmation evidence (Pass 1):** Owner earnings (FCF − SBC) positive and $1B+; ROIC holding at or above 15%; subscription gross margin compressing modestly but remaining above 75%.

**Break evidence (Pass 1):** Owner earnings near zero or negative; ROIC declining toward 10%; post-Armis debt materially higher than 0.4x Debt/OCF suggests; SBC as % of revenue not declining.

**Q20. Pass 1 focus questions:**

1. **Owner earnings**: FCF TTM minus SBC TTM — is NOW generating positive economic earnings, or is adjusted EPS an accounting construct?
2. **SBC trend**: Is SBC as % of revenue declining as management aspirationally guides, or holding flat/rising? Is there a normalization trajectory?
3. **Subscription gross margin trajectory**: What is the quarterly trend in subscription gross margin? Is the management-guided compression already accelerating, and how much room remains before it becomes a structural concern?
4. **Post-Armis debt and leverage**: What does the balance sheet look like post-$7.75B Armis close? Does Debt/OCF at 0.4x already reflect Armis, or is this a pre-close figure?
5. **ROIC quality**: Is the 15.4% ROIC holding or deteriorating? How is goodwill from Armis/Moveworks inflating the invested capital denominator?
