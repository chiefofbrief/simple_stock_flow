## 1. What drove results this quarter?

> **Q1 2026 Financial Performance Summary (10-Q, three months ended March 31, 2026):**
> Revenue: $15,917M (2026) vs. $14,541M (2025); +9.5% as reported, +6.1% adjusted for currency
> Gross profit margin: 56.2% (2026) vs. 55.2% (2025); +1.0 pts
> Total expense and other (income): $7,562M vs. $6,873M; +10.0%
> Income from continuing operations before income taxes: $1,387M vs. $1,158M; +19.8%
> Provision for income taxes (continuing operations): $172M vs. $103M; +65.8%
> Income from continuing operations: $1,216M vs. $1,054M; +15.3%
> Income from continuing operations margin: 7.6% vs. 7.3%; +0.4 pts
> Net income: $1,216M vs. $1,055M; +15.2%
> EPS from continuing operations (diluted): $1.28 vs. $1.12; +14.3%
> Operating (non-GAAP) EPS (diluted): $1.91 vs. $1.60; +19.4%
> Cash from operations: $5.2 billion; Free cash flow: $2.2 billion
> Returned $1.6 billion to shareholders in dividends.
>
> Total revenue grew 9.5 percent as reported and 6.1 percent adjusted for currency compared to the prior-year period. Software delivered revenue growth of 11.3 percent as reported (7.9 percent adjusted for currency). Consulting revenue increased 4.0 percent as reported (0.9 percent adjusted for currency). Infrastructure revenue increased 15.3 percent as reported (11.7 percent adjusted for currency).
>
> From a geographic perspective, Americas revenue increased 9.1 percent as reported (8.2 percent adjusted for currency). EMEA increased 15.2 percent as reported (5.4 percent adjusted for currency). Asia Pacific increased 1.1 percent as reported (1.7 percent adjusted for currency).
>
> Gross margin of 56.2 percent increased 1.0 point year to year with margin expansion driven primarily by productivity actions, revenue growth and portfolio mix. Operating (non-GAAP) gross margin of 57.7 percent increased 1.1 points.
>
> Total expense and other (income) increased 10.0 percent in Q1 2026 driven by our organic and inorganic investments in portfolio innovation and the effects of currency, partially offset by savings from productivity actions.
>
> Pre-tax margin was up 0.8 points year to year to 8.7 percent. Operating (non-GAAP) pre-tax income of $2.1 billion increased 22.5 percent and operating (non-GAAP) pre-tax margin increased 1.4 points to 13.4 percent.
>
> The year-to-year performance was primarily driven by revenue growth, portfolio mix and increased productivity, partially offset by our organic and inorganic investments in portfolio innovation.
>
> Balance sheet: Cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash and marketable securities at March 31, 2026 of $11.8 billion decreased $2.6 billion from December 31, 2025. Debt of $66.4 billion increased $5.1 billion (primarily driven by Q1 2026 debt issuances to increase financial liquidity and plan for future debt maturities).
>
> **Key acquisition — Q1 2026:**
> On March 17, 2026, IBM completed the acquisition of Confluent, Inc. IBM's and Confluent's combined portfolios enable enterprises to deploy generative and agentic AI better and faster by providing trusted communication and data flow between environments, applications and APIs. Goodwill of $7,136M (Software) and $89M (Consulting) assigned. Weighted-average useful life of identifiable amortizable intangibles: 9.7 years. IBM issued and assumed 3.0M stock awards with fair value of $665M.

## 3. Management guidance — where is the business going?

> Interim results are not necessarily indicative of financial results for a full year. The information included in this Form 10-Q should be read in conjunction with the company's 2025 Annual Report.
>
> **Macroeconomic Environment:**
> The strength of our portfolio and the resiliency of our business model, underpinned by our software-led hybrid cloud and AI strategy, position us well to navigate the current climate. While the economic and geopolitical environment remain dynamic and uncertain, businesses continue to invest in technology to scale AI, drive productivity, increase resiliency and accelerate their growth. This was reflected in our performance in the first quarter. Our durable, high value portfolio enables us to execute on our strategy delivering innovation to our clients and partners.
>
> **Looking Forward (from MD&A):**
> Technology remains a key driver of growth and competitive advantage which allows businesses to scale, drive cost efficiencies, productivity and transformation. It is clear that hybrid cloud and AI are the two most consequential technologies for enterprise performance. These technologies are no longer viewed as incremental tools, but as platforms that fundamentally change how businesses scale, compete, and operate. Their value is even more critical in today's environment.
>
> AI is changing the economics of enterprise operations. To capture this opportunity and build a competitive advantage, businesses must go beyond just adding AI; they must become AI-first. The portfolio of AI offerings we have built, including cost efficient, fit-for-purpose open-source models deployed in hybrid environments, is focused on helping businesses scale AI and generate return through productivity improvements and automation.
>
> **R&D expense:**
> R&D expense increased 11.4 percent in Q1 2026. The year-to-year increase reflects our organic and inorganic investments to drive innovation in AI, hybrid cloud and quantum, and the effects of currency.
>
> **SG&A expense:**
> Total SG&A expense increased 4.2 percent in Q1 2026, driven primarily by: higher operating expenses from acquired businesses (3 pts), effects of currency (2 pts), higher acquisition-related charges and amortization of acquired intangibles (1 pt); partially offset by benefits from productivity and operational transformation actions (2 pts).
>
> **Currency:**
> Movements in currency, and the fact that we do not hedge 100% of our currency exposures, will result in a currency impact to revenues, profit and cash flows throughout 2026. We execute a hedging program which defers, versus eliminates, the volatility of currency impacts.
>
> **Capital:**
> The balance sheet continues to provide financial flexibility to support and invest in the business. Total debt of $66.4 billion increased $5.1 billion in Q1 2026, primarily driven by Q1 2026 debt issuances to increase financial liquidity and plan for future debt maturities.

## 4. Risks and headwinds flagged by management

> **Macroeconomic and geopolitical:**
> The economic and geopolitical environment remain dynamic and uncertain. In the first three months of 2026, movements in global currencies continued to impact our reported year-to-year revenue and profit. We execute hedging programs which defer, but do not eliminate, the impact of currency.
>
> **Consulting margin headwinds:**
> Consulting segment profit and profit margin performance in Q1 2026 compared to the prior-year period declined modestly as productivity gains were offset by investments in the business and currency headwinds reflecting our geographic mix of the business.
>
> **IBM tax:**
> The U.S. IRS has proposed adjustments related to certain cross-border transactions with respect to the company's 2013-2014 and 2015-2016 U.S. income tax returns. The company strongly disagrees with the IRS' proposed adjustments and will pursue resolution at court if necessary. In Q4 2025, the IRS concluded its audit of the company's 2017-2018 U.S. income tax returns. The company strongly disagrees with certain adjustments proposed by the IRS and is evaluating its options to contest them.
>
> IBM's tax provision and effective tax rate are impacted by recurring and discrete factors including the geographical mix of income before taxes, changes in business operations, incentives, specific transactions, changes in unrecognized tax benefits, settlement of income tax audits, and changes in tax laws or regulations.
>
> **Infrastructure cycle risk:**
> IBM Z revenue is driven by product cycles (z17 launched June 2025). Infrastructure Support decreased 1.8% as reported. Segment performance is subject to IBM Z product cycle dynamics.
>
> **Litigation exposure:**
> IBM is involved, either as plaintiff or defendant, in a variety of ongoing claims, demands, suits, investigations, tax matters and proceedings. Given the rapidly evolving external landscape of cybersecurity, AI, privacy and data protection laws and threat actors, the company and its clients have been and will continue to be subject to actions or proceedings in various jurisdictions.
>
> **Partially offsetting factors noted:**
> Total expense and other (income) increased 10.0 percent, partially offset by savings from productivity actions. Total operating (non-GAAP) expense increased 8.7 percent year to year. Expected credit loss expense was a benefit of $2M in Q1 2026 vs. a provision of $14M in prior year (driven by higher unallocated reserve requirements in prior year due to economic conditions).

## 5. Critical Accounting Estimates

> Critical Accounting Estimates are incorporated by reference to IBM's 2025 Annual Report to Stockholders (pages 6–38) and are not reproduced in the SEC 10-K filing. Not found in the 10-Q MD&A either.
>
> **10-Q accounting changes disclosed (Note 2):**
>
> New Standards to be Implemented:
>
> Intangibles — Goodwill and Other — Internal-Use Software (issued September 2025): This guidance requires internal-use software development cost capitalization to begin when both of the following occur: management has authorized and committed to funding the software project and it is probable the project will be completed and the software will be used to perform its intended function. This guidance eliminates accounting considerations of software development stages. Effective date: January 1, 2028 (annual and interim). Early adoption permitted. The company is evaluating the impact.
>
> Disaggregation of Income Statement Expenses (issued November 2024): Requires new tabular disclosure of certain types of expenses (purchases of inventory, employee compensation, D&A) included within commonly presented expense captions. Also requires disclosure of total selling expenses and their definition. Effective for annual periods beginning 2027; interim periods beginning January 1, 2028. Early adoption permitted. The company expects to adopt as of the effective date on a prospective basis. This is a change to disclosures only and will not impact consolidated financial results.
>
> **Revenue Recognition — RPO practical expedient:**
> As a practical expedient, the company does not include contracts that have an original duration of one year or less in RPO disclosure. RPO estimates are subject to change and are affected by several factors, including terminations, changes in the scope of contracts, periodic revalidations, adjustment for revenue not materialized and adjustments for currency.

## 6. AI investment, pricing model, and monetization

> **Strategic positioning:**
> Our first-quarter performance reinforces the strategic choices we have made over the last several years to advance IBM as a software-led Hybrid Cloud and AI platform company.
>
> AI is changing the economics of enterprise operations. To capture this opportunity and build a competitive advantage, businesses must go beyond just adding AI; they must become AI-first. The portfolio of AI offerings we have built, including cost efficient, fit-for-purpose open-source models deployed in hybrid environments, is focused on helping businesses scale AI and generate return through productivity improvements and automation.
>
> In Software, IBM watsonx provides a robust portfolio of AI products for developing AI apps, managing data, and governing the entire lifecycle of AI models and AI agents, allowing clients to move from pilots to production with full control over cost, security, sovereignty, and performance. Our watsonx platform and watsonx Orchestrate help enterprises deploy AI by connecting agents, models, and workflows with governance and security.
>
> In Consulting, AI is both a growth driver and a productivity engine. Our experts are helping clients design and execute AI strategies by leveraging the IBM Consulting Advantage platform, an AI delivery platform designed to implement solutions at scale, transforming how our consultants work. As agents take on more work, delivery becomes faster, more software driven, and more scalable.
>
> Infrastructure plays a critical role as AI moves into the core of enterprise operations, enabling hybrid cloud environments for mission-critical transactions and AI workloads. IBM Z delivers enhanced AI acceleration through multi-model AI capabilities, low unit cost architecture at scale for workloads that require end-to-end encryption, continued availability, and ultra-high throughput.
>
> **Client Zero / internal AI deployment:**
> We are transforming our enterprise operations, driving efficiency and cost savings with our Client Zero approach, leveraging technology and embedding AI in our own workflows. IBM Bob, our AI-based software development system that automates the full software lifecycle, became generally available in March 2026, driving developer productivity and predictable enterprise costs.
>
> **AI monetization — Software metrics:**
> Software ARR: $24.6 billion as of Q1 2026, up approximately $3 billion as reported YoY. ARR includes: (1) software subscription agreements including committed term licenses, (2) as-a-service arrangements (SaaS and PaaS), and (3) maintenance and support contracts.
>
> Data revenue grew 19.2% as reported reflecting demand for our generative AI products, strength in our strategic partnerships, and inorganic contribution from acquisitions (DataStax and Confluent).
>
> **RPO:**
> At March 31, 2026, RPO was approximately $69 billion. Approximately 69% expected to be recognized as revenue in the subsequent two years, approximately 28% in the subsequent three to five years, and the balance thereafter.
>
> **Consulting backlog:**
> Consulting backlog at March 31, 2026: $31.3 billion. Book-to-bill (trailing twelve months): 1.04. Consulting signings: $5,354M in Q1 2026, +8.5% as reported (+6.0% at constant currency).
>
> **AI-related acquisitions:**
> - Confluent, Inc. (closed March 17, 2026): enables enterprises to deploy generative and agentic AI by providing trusted communication and data flow between environments, applications and APIs. Purchase consideration not separately disclosed in 10-Q excerpts but goodwill assigned: $7,136M (Software) + $89M (Consulting).
> - HashiCorp (closed February 2025): included in Automation revenue growth.
> - DataStax: contributing to Data revenue growth.
>
> **R&D investment:**
> R&D expense increased 11.4 percent in Q1 2026, reflecting organic and inorganic investments to drive innovation in AI, hybrid cloud and quantum, and the effects of currency.
>
> SG&A increased 4.2 percent, with 3 points driven by higher operating expenses from acquired businesses as a result of continued investment to drive the hybrid cloud and AI strategy.
