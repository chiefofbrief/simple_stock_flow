#### Section 6: Preliminary Hypothesis

**Q19. Preliminary hypothesis.**

**Numbers**
Pass 1 will likely confirm: (1) OCF growing at ~11% CAGR but FCF structurally negative (-$400M FY2025) due to $4.1B+ CapEx far exceeding depreciation; (2) ROIC of ~5% on the full asset base (low by traditional standards, but driven by massive PP&E denominator from a REIT's capital-intensive model); (3) 26% cash-on-cash returns on stabilized assets, consistent with management guidance; (4) Debt/OCF of ~6x (elevated but stable, supported by recurring revenue quality); (5) AFFO growing at ~10-12% YoY from a base of ~$10.79/quarter. The core financial picture is: durable recurring revenue quality, high capex intensity, thin GAAP earnings, better AFFO story, negative near-term FCF during the growth buildout.

**Narrative & Catalyst**
A story is forming clearly and from institutional sources — 94.94% institutional ownership, analyst upgrade cycle complete post-Q1 2026, Distributed AI Hub generating third-party ecosystem coverage (Tenstorrent), new product launches (Fabric Intelligence) receiving favorable analyst commentary. The Fabric Intelligence / Distributed AI Hub narrative is the new narrative layer, distinct from the base colocation story. This is moving in the right direction but is not yet a dominant re-rating catalyst. Path to price realization: Q2-Q3 2026 AFFO acceleration + first evidence of interconnection growing faster than colocation revenue. Timeframe: 12-18 months for base case; 24-36 months for full platform re-rating.

**Scenario**
Current price ($1,068) appears to embed the **BASE CASE**: recurring revenue growing 10-12% constant currency, AFFO per share reaching $43-46 in 2026, traded at 23-25x forward AFFO. This is consistent with a quality infrastructure compounder at fair value.

Bull case (**NOT priced in**): AFFO growth accelerates to 14-16% from interconnection premium pricing + Distributed AI Hub platform monetization; multiple re-rates to 28-32x AFFO as narrative shifts from REIT to intelligent infrastructure → price $1,300-1,450.

Bear case (**NOT priced in**): Power procurement failures constrain expansion in key markets; AFFO growth slows to 5-7%; multiple compresses to 18-20x AFFO → price $800-850.

**Thesis**
EQIX is a structurally positioned LEVERAGED L9 AI infrastructure operator with a genuine carrier-neutral interconnection moat and record AI-driven booking momentum. The Distributed AI Hub / Fabric Intelligence represents a potential platform evolution that is not yet in revenue but is credible and beginning to attract third-party ecosystem integrations. However, current price reflects the base case AI tailwind scenario with only modest additional upside to analyst consensus (+9.8%). The stock is not cheap. Expected value at current price is approximately **dollar for a dollar** — quality at full price — with asymmetric upside optionality from interconnection platform monetization, and meaningful downside exposure if power/execution challenges impair the AFFO growth trajectory.

**Confirms thesis:** Q2 2026 AFFO at or above $11.00/share; interconnection revenue growing faster than colocation constant currency; any named ExScale pre-leasing commitment above $200M.

**Breaks thesis:** AFFO growth deceleration below 8% for two consecutive quarters; power procurement failures causing lease cancellations or revenue deferrals; major hyperscaler customer consolidating colocation into owned or DLR-managed facilities; AFFO guidance cut at any point in 2026.
