# Analyst Q&A Questions: APP
**Quarter:** Q2 FY2026 (fiscal quarter ended June 30, 2026)
**Call date:** August 5, 2026
**Source:** Extracted from user-provided transcript (ASR), saved 2026-08-06

1. **Jason Bazinet (Citi)** — First time hearing about partners to bring in more customers; expand on the partnership opportunity. [Follow-up: "So this is like Triple Whale?"]

2. **James Heaney (Jefferies)** — Under the hood of the gaming advertising business: exactly what model breakthroughs were you looking for that didn't happen, and the timing of what happened in the quarter? [Follow-up: update on consumer vertical progress in Q2 — how much growth came from existing customers vs. new advertisers?]

3. **Stephen Ju (UBS)** — E-commerce advertisers say they're nowhere near hitting ROAS ceilings; is that a positively biased group or what you're hearing broadly? And has your perspective on working with larger advertisers changed in the last 3–6 months, since some mobile game advertisers are also large?

4. **Ralph Schackart (William Blair)** — Any sense of why you didn't get the model uplift you'd have expected in the quarter, and a way to diagnose it? Rate of improvement post-quarter? [For Matt: as compute costs increase in scale, how should we think about the margin profile of the business?]

5. **Omar Dessouky (Bank of America)** — Platform opened to everyone in late June (~6 weeks): how do you evaluate onboarding performance (# advertisers onboarded; met/exceeded/below expectations), and trajectory for the rest of the year into CY27? Is consumer a "tens of thousands spending a little" or "few spending a lot" business, and how long targeted vs. advertisers flocking (like Meta/Google)? [Follow-up: which specific parts of your technology are ahead of / behind schedule?]

6. **Rob Sanderson (Loop Capital)** — Self-service funnel: earlier ~57% of qualified leads coming through with creative leakage the main issue — how is that changing now that you have ad-creation tools? And feedback from commerce advertisers now that you're GA — what's working, what needs improvement, and common feature requests (targeting, measurement, campaign automation)?

7. **Alec Brondolo (Wells Fargo)** — Health of the mobile game ecosystem: CPI inflation, waning ROAS, downloads down ~10–15% YoY — how do you feel about the category and how are customer conversations progressing? [Follow-up: web advertising branding — AppLovin vs. Axon; does lack of brand awareness hurt your ability to acquire the next 100,000 long-tail advertisers?]

8. **Robert Coolbrith (Evercore)** — Advertisers spending more on Android than expected (Play ecosystem dynamics) potentially affecting share of wallet — opportunity within Android to drive competitive distance and higher share over time? And the World Cup as a possible headwind for IAP advertisers / other cyclical Q2 dynamics?

9. **Jim Callahan (Piper Sandler)** — Revenue by geography: U.S. accelerated Q/Q but international closer to flat vs. 1Q — anything to call out on the delta? [Follow-up: non-gaming excluding e-com — verticals like short dramas or prediction markets leaning into spend?]

10. **Matt Swanson (RBC)** — You've built the company very lean; what are the advantages of going from dozens to hundreds of A/B test iterations? Is there a right level of R&D spend / diminishing return from ramping?

11. **Clark Lampen (BTIG)** — On the data-collection layer: anything you can do (ad credits, more of a managed-service presence) to help advertisers scale volume and feed data, and does that help the customer-growth lever? And a brief update on the lead-gen business — is it ramping / directional trends?

12. **Aaron Lee (Macquarie)** — Incremental investment in tech/compute going forward beyond 3Q — should we expect an elevated investment runway, and how much visibility into training/compute costs? [Follow-up: is the ~$70,000 first-year gross-spend-per-new-customer figure still the right bogey, and are model breakthroughs the key driver to raising it?]

13. **Martin Yang (OpCo)** — Nature of the partnerships: attribution partners giving customers additional credit / better data integration — what do the partners' customers benefit from with AppLovin? [Follow-up: moving beyond mid-market to Fortune 500 — quality-control concerns and would you expand supply to non-gaming?]

14. **Vasily Karasyov (Cannonball)** — Long-term: run-time data integration with Unity Vector — engine-level signals you may not have native access to; could the channels be complementary (different signal sets)? What does this new signal set mean for the industry — does overall spend grow?

15. **Tim Nollen (SSR)** — Now that consumer is GA, are you turning more attention to Wurl / CTV? How does the consumer experience lend itself to Wurl (in-game ads → CTV ads)?
