# Investment Thesis: RDDT

### Discovery Signal

**Flagged:** Reached record traffic of 5.76B in March; positioning itself as a primary source for LLM training data (Source: r/ValueInvesting).
**Sector:** Communication Services
**Market Cap:** $29.48B
**Description:** Operates a network of digital communities. The company's unique, human-generated conversational data is becoming a critical "gold mine" for companies training Large Language Models.
**Catalysts:** 29% surge in monthly traffic; licensing deals with AI giants; positioning as the "front page" of human AI training data.
**Peers:** META, GOOGL, PINS, SNAP.
**Source:** News: Reddit(RDDT) saw record-breaking monthly traffic in March [r/ValueInvesting]

### Price
PASS. Tailwind. At $147.93 (Apr 28), RDDT trades +22.1% above its level from one year ago but sits at only ~30% of its 52-week range ($90.78–$282.95) — confirming the stock remains deep in consolidation territory after its speculative peak near $283. The 52-week position has compressed slightly since the Apr 14 screening (33% → 30%), consistent with the market's continued rotation out of high-multiple growth names. With earnings due Apr 30, the stock faces binary event risk into the print; a negative forward estimate (see Earnings section) elevates that risk. Price PASS holds — the tailwind entry thesis remains intact, but position sizing ahead of the print carries elevated uncertainty.

### Earnings
PASS — with caution on earnings reliability. Updated Apr 28. RDDT's multiple has compressed further (140x → 59x → 55.1x GAAP, Apr 28) and EPS YoY growth remains +238.5%, with revenue up +69.7% and FCF up +195.7% YoY. The surface case continues to improve directionally. However, the core concern from the Apr 14 analysis is unchanged: TTM EPS of ~$2.69 (implied at 55.1x GAAP P/E, $147.93 price) from a company with only 1/5 years of profitability is not a stable earnings base — it is a base-effect artifact of first-year profitability, and the 4-quarter avg EPS QoQ of +77.9% reflects growth from a near-zero base rather than durable compounding.

The pre-earnings forward delta of **-$0.62** (Q1 2026 analyst estimate vs. Q4 2025 actual) is a new and important risk flag: analysts expect a meaningful sequential step-down into the print. This is plausible on seasonal grounds (Q1 is the weakest advertising quarter), but it means the Apr 30 print will be evaluated against elevated expectations set by a strong Q4 — a harder bar to clear. A miss or cautious guidance could amplify multiple expansion risk at 55x.

The thesis passes on the strength of continued multiple compression and the data licensing tailwind, but the Apr 30 print is an immediate and material test. Conviction in the earnings base requires evidence that Q1 2026 delivers sustainable GAAP profitability — not just beats against a lowered forward estimate.

### Financials
FILTERED. Analysis date: 2026-04-29. Peer: META.

RDDT's structural model is genuinely excellent: near-zero CapEx (FCF ≈ OCF), operating leverage emerging (2.58× annual, 2.80× in Q4 2025), effectively debt-free (Debt/OCF 0.03×), and a cash-rich balance sheet ($2.86B WC vs. $2.20B TTM revenue). Revenue is compounding at 46% CAGR and accelerating (+69.4% in 2025). These are the right ingredients for a long-term compounder.

The financials fail on one central question: **do they validate the 238.5% EPS growth — and does this justify the multiple?**

The answer is no, on both counts. The EPS surge has three components, only one of which represents durable compounding: (1) revenue growth (+69.4%) — real and validated; (2) SBC normalization, from $0.80B (61.7% of revenue) in the IPO year to $0.34B (15.6%) in 2025 — a one-time base effect, not repeating earnings growth; (3) operating leverage from crossing break-even — real, but the most powerful leverage moment is the transition from negative to zero, not the continuation. Stripping the SBC normalization artifact, the underlying earnings improvement is significant but not sufficient to anchor the multiple.

The honest valuation: owner earnings = FCF − SBC = $0.68B − $0.34B = **~$0.34B**, implying **~83× owner earnings** at current market cap ($28.26B). That is the actual price of ownership — 2× the GAAP P/E of 55×, and priced for years of compounding that have not been demonstrated. The business has **1/5 years of GAAP profitability**, with 2024 being a deeply unprofitable IPO year and 2025 being the first genuine year of profit. There is no proven track record through a full cycle, and no demonstrated ability to sustain profitability through Q1 seasonal weakness (Q1 2025 operating margin: 1.0%).

Footnotes cannot rescue this. The SBC run rate (~$0.09B/quarter) is already visible in the quarterly data. Even in the best-case scenario — SBC normalizes to META's 10% of revenue — owner earnings rise to ~$0.46B, still implying ~61× at current prices. The valuation requires the business to prove its earnings base over multiple quarters before the multiple is justified.

**Re-entry signal:** Two to three consecutive quarters of clean GAAP profitability that survive Q1 seasonal compression, with SBC/Revenue continuing to decline toward META's 10% level, and evidence that data licensing revenue is contributing at material scale. The Apr 30 earnings print is the first data point in this sequence — not the conclusion. A Q1 2026 EPS materially above Q1 2025 (~$0) on a year-over-year basis would be the earliest signal worth monitoring.
