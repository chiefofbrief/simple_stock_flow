# Investment Thesis: RDDT

### Discovery Signal
**[TAILWIND] — LLM Training Data & Community Engagement**
**Flagged:** Reached record traffic of 5.76B in March; positioning itself as a primary source for LLM training data (Source: r/ValueInvesting).
**Sector:** Communication Services
**Market Cap:** $29.48B
**Description:** Operates a network of digital communities. The company's unique, human-generated conversational data is becoming a critical "gold mine" for companies training Large Language Models.
**Catalysts:** 29% surge in monthly traffic; licensing deals with AI giants; positioning as the "front page" of human AI training data.
**Peers:** META, GOOGL, PINS, SNAP.
**Source:** News: Reddit(RDDT) saw record-breaking monthly traffic in March [r/ValueInvesting]

### Price
PASS. Tailwind. RDDT is currently in a healthy consolidation phase after a speculative surge in 2025. Trading at just 33% of its 52-week range and +17% below its 1-year mean, the stock offers a potential entry point for the LLM training data tailwind. The recent April recovery (+15%) indicates a floor may be forming after the Q1 sell-off.

### Earnings
PASS — with caution on earnings reliability. RDDT's multiple compression (140x → 59x) and +249% TTM EPS growth make the surface case compelling. However, $2.62 TTM EPS from a company that was loss-making three years ago with a CV of 7.10 is not a stable earnings base — it is a first-year-of-profitability data point. At 58.9x, the multiple is similar to AMD's (61.2x), which was filtered on valuation grounds; the distinction is directional (multiple compressing vs. expanding), but that alone does not resolve the underlying earnings fragility. The thesis passes on the strength of the data licensing tailwind and multiple compression, but must be treated as a provisional pass pending evidence that the $2.62 earnings level is sustainable rather than a base-effect artifact. The 2026-04-30 earnings print is an immediate test.
