**Q3. Where does sentiment sit in the cycle?**

The AI narrative has driven AVGO for 2+ years, since Broadcom's custom ASIC thesis emerged in 2022–23 and accelerated through the hyperscaler buildout of 2024–2025. The stock has compounded at 56.2% per year over 5 years. [CONFIRMED: AVGO_price.json]

This is the hallmark of a reflexivity-adjacent situation: the AI thesis is structurally real AND sentiment has built a premium beyond what current fundamentals alone justify.

Direction: Sentiment is simultaneously (a) validated by genuine fundamental progress and (b) extended beyond what current-year earnings support. The April 2026 price surge (+34.9% in a single month) following the Google/Meta contract announcements — both of which were contract extensions rather than new business — illustrates how sentiment is amplifying already-known structural advantages. The earnings cycle (June 3) is the next sentiment test.

**Reflexivity note:** Broadcom's rising market cap and credibility have reinforced its ability to attract and retain hyperscaler clients and recruit talent — a mild reflexivity loop. However, the primary reflexivity mechanism (can the stock price affect fundamentals?) is weaker here than in, say, a company using stock as acquisition currency. Broadcom's semiconductor business depends on chip design excellence, not capital markets dynamics.

**Reflexivity + AI Lifecycle**
