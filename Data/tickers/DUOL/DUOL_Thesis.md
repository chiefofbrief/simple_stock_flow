#### Section 1: Sentiment Landscape

**Q1. Mainstream Narrative**

The dominant market concern is AI disruption: will large language models (ChatGPT, Gemini) make dedicated language-learning apps redundant? This narrative has been the primary driver of DUOL's price collapse since mid-2024. News coverage concentrated on: (1) AI disruption risk to the core business model [INFERRED: multiple Perigon/FMP stories, April–May 2026]; (2) management's deliberate monetization slowdown announced at Q1 2026 earnings (May 4, 2026) — guiding to ~5.8% Q2 bookings growth vs. 33% prior year [CONFIRMED: Perigon, "Duolingo Q1 Earnings Beat as Guidance Slows Monetization," 2026-05-07]; (3) Q1 2026 earnings beat on revenue (+27% YoY to $292M) and EPS ($0.89 vs. $0.79 estimated) but stock fell ~14% after hours on guidance [CONFIRMED: Zacks, "Duolingo, Inc. (DUOL) Q1 Earnings and Revenues Surpass Estimates," 2026-05-04]; (4) CEO backlash over April 2025 AI-first memo that drove user deletions and employee resistance [CONFIRMED: Perigon, "Duolingo CEO stops AI-use performance reviews," 2026-04-15].

Analyst Q&A questions reflect three concentrated concerns: (1) What is driving MAU deceleration (MAU growth fell to 5.8% YoY from 14.1% prior quarter, while DAU grew 21%)? [CONFIRMED: DUOL_qa_questions.md, 2026Q1 Q4 — Boone]; (2) Can the engagement-over-monetization strategy produce a credible back-half bookings reacceleration? [CONFIRMED: DUOL_qa_questions.md, 2026Q1 Q8 — Smilek, Q21 — Sklar]; (3) What is the real threat from AI competitors and can management sustain product quality amid AI-accelerated content creation? [CONFIRMED: DUOL_qa_questions.md, 2025Q4 Q12 — Feather, 2026Q1 Q6 — Sheridan, Q17 — Khajuria].

Gap assessment: The market is primarily reacting to AI disruption *fear* and management's own guidance signal — not to demonstrated fundamental deterioration. Q1 EPS of $0.89 beat estimates by 12.7%; gross margins improved from 71.1% to 73.0%; operating income grew 89% YoY. The negative sentiment is real (stock fell 14% on a quarter that beat estimates) but is grounded in forward uncertainty, not backward-looking results. Management's April 2025 AI memo is the only confirmed case where narrative harmed the actual business (admitted in the 10-Q) [CONFIRMED: DUOL_mda_excerpts.md, Risk Section 4]. This is a meaningful counterpoint to the purely "overreaction" narrative — the company caused some self-inflicted damage.

Counterpoint to the bull case: The MAU deceleration is real. MAU growth fell from 14.1% to 5.8% YoY in Q1 2026 [CONFIRMED: news, DA Davidson commentary, 2026-05-08]. The gap between DAU growth (21%) and MAU growth (5.8%) is widening — DAUs are growing faster than the overall user base, meaning the user base itself is not expanding as fast as the engaged sub-set. This is both a positive sign (existing users engaging more) and a concern (top-of-funnel is slowing).

Today, at $108.35, the stock trades at 29.36x TTM EPS of $3.69 — after EPS has nearly quadrupled from the 2024 peak levels. [ESTIMATED: derivation of P/E from price/EPS]. The market has gone from pricing in extraordinary growth at 200x+ to pricing in potential structural decline at ~29x.

The central question for Pass 1 is whether the 29.36x represents fair value, cheap, or expensive — accounting for the forward earnings deceleration management has guided.
