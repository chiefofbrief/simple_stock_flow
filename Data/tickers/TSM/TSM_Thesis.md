TSMC Q1 2026 earnings beat (net income +58% YoY, revenue +35% YoY) drove a rally that pushed market cap past $2 trillion [CONFIRMED: Perigon, 2026-04-29].

**Analyst Q&A questions confirm** the professional community is concentrated on: supply constraint duration and capacity ramp pace; N3 gross margin crossover timing and depreciation impact post-full-depreciation; CapEx trajectory vs revenue growth; margin sustainability at 66%+; Terafab/competitive threats from customers building own fabs; Arizona fab economics vs Taiwan; AI revenue forecasting methodology; advanced packaging (CoWoS/SoIC/CoPoS) strategy; and potential Samsung LPU wins. **Operating Margin**

Operating margin expanded from 40.9% (2021) to 58.0% (Q1 2026, Gemini; 58.1% per official), a 17pp improvement in 5 years [CONFIRMED: `TSM_financial_analysis.md`]. CV of 0.09 on the annual series — one of the lowest in the table, indicating a stable upward trend despite the 2023 trough (42.6%). Quarterly progression over the past 4 quarters is striking: 49.6% → 50.6% → 53.9% → 58.0% — accelerating, not decelerating. Operating expenses have held at 8.3–8.4% of revenue, so virtually all margin improvement is gross margin expansion (utilization, node mix, FX).

Two headwinds are confirmed and quantified: (1) N2 ramp will dilute gross margin 2–3% for full-year 2026 [CONFIRMED: earnings call data used in Context step]; (2) overseas fabs diluting 2–3% currently, widening to 3–4%. Management's explicit long-term gross margin target is "56% and higher through the cycle" — approximately 10pp below Q1 2026 actuals. This is not a hidden risk; it is fully disclosed. The question is pace of compression, not direction.

Additional mechanism flagged from footnotes: when fab utilization exceeds "normal capacity," TSMC's inventory policy reduces inventory cost per unit (overhead absorption benefit). At current near-100% utilization on N3, this is actively reducing Cost of Revenue per wafer, contributing to the 66% gross margin. When utilization eventually normalizes — either from demand softening or new capacity coming online — this overhead absorption benefit mechanically reverses and adds to Cost of Revenue. [CONFIRMED: `Footnotes_2025_Annual_report.md`, Note 5, Inventory Valuation policy; `TSM_mda_excerpts.md` confirms above-normal capacity utilization methodology]

Peer: AMAT operating margin 29–30% (CV 0.02) — highly stable but significantly lower. TSM's superior margin reflects its monopoly at the leading edge, not cost efficiency relative to peers.

**What the calls add that financial statements couldn't:**

**Notable omission:** Management does not disclose what share of the current margin outperformance vs. the 56% floor is attributable to utilization (overhead absorption) vs. N3/N5 mix vs. pricing vs. cost improvement.
