## II. [cite_start]Profit & Expense Analysis [cite: 83]
[cite_start]Page 2 [cite: 84]

[cite_start]Revenue Analysis: [cite: 85]
[cite_start]In the first quarter, revenue increased 8.4% quarter-over-quarter, as our business was supported by strong demand for our leading-edge process technologies. [cite: 86]
[cite_start]By technology, 3nm process technology contributed 25% of total wafer revenue in 1Q26 while 5nm and 7nm accounted for 36% and 13% respectively. [cite: 87]
[cite_start]Advanced technologies (7nm and below) accounted for 74% of total wafer revenue. [cite: 88]
[cite_start]By platform, HPC and Smartphone represented 61% and 26% of net revenue respectively, while loT, Automotive, DCE, and Others each represented 6%, 4%, 1%, and 2%. [cite: 89]
[cite_start]Sequentially, revenue from HPC, IoT, DCE and Others increased 20%, 12%, 28%, and 17% respectively. [cite: 90]
[cite_start]Smartphone and Automotive decreased 11% and 7% respectively from 4Q25. [cite: 91]
[cite_start]From a geographic perspective, revenue from customers based in North America accounted for 76% of total net revenue in 1Q26, while revenue from Asia Pacific, China, Japan, and EMEA (Europe, Middle East, and Africa) accounted for 9%, 7%, 4%, and 4% of total net revenue respectively.
