## CURRENT QUARTER: 2026Q4

**Q1 — Yoshida**
Yes, I am Yoshida from Nikkei. I have two questions. The first question is about the establishment of the joint venture with TSMC, which was just announced in the equity market, and the low synergy with the entertainment area. So the possibility of a spin-out has been pointed out. But Sony, so this MOU agreement is based on Sony being the major or controlling shareholder. So I would like to hear the background. And I would like to hear from Totoki-san about the stock prices. With the surge of semiconductor memory prices and not being seen as an AI title, the stock prices have been going down these few days and the stock prices have not really made a rebound. But based on what you have just explained, do you think you now have a good explanation to the market?

**Q2 — Tamura**
For myself, I'd like to ask about the business you have in the United States. And you had alluded to adaptability earlier, Totoki-san. In the United States, there has been the tariffs issue. So 10% tariffs, it has been decided that it is illegal, so in the United States there is uncertainty regarding the direction of tariffs. So about the U.S. tariff situation, how do you see it? Especially after this court judgment about the tariffs.

**Q3 — Analyst**
Two questions. At the joint venture with TSMC, I think the logic is that TSMC will handle the foundry and Sony will do the image design and finishing. But regarding the pixel part, I think your differentiating capability is the image capability. You have been talking about the difficulty of achieving very precise images and fabrication precision. With the joint venture, is there not a concern that this will be a challenge? What is your expectation towards the joint venture? Is it a financial expectation or other strategic expectations? That's my first question. And the second question is about AI and the increase in content. There will be a race over capturing users' time. Location-based entertainment (LBE) and other entertainment experiences are also important. What is your take on this currently?

**Q4 — Nishita**
I have two questions. The first question is about Sony and Honda. Since this is accounted for under the equity method, do you intend to ask Honda to bear more burden due to the change in business? And secondly, about the memory shortage: there could be a big impact on gaming consoles. For the industry, there might be a supply problem because memory prices are going up, causing console prices to rise. Maybe the PS5 will be fine, but looking at the coming 1 to 3 years, how would gaming console prices be impacted? Could you give us a breakdown?

**Q5 — Goto**
My name is Goto from TV Tokyo. I'd like to ask Totoki-san as CEO: about price increases, you said that PlayStation 5 pricing is fine and you are not going to increase the price. That's the first question. The second question is about the expected business results. There is uncertainty in the geopolitical situation, yet you expect record high net profit. How do you assess the risks underlying this forecast? What assumptions do you have to make this forecast?

**Q6 — Mikio Hirakawa**
Hirakawa from BofA Securities. Two questions. First about AI. Second is about mobility. First, on AI: as you've explained, platforms will become more important in AI and I think this will appeal to people's emotions. But for other areas like piracy, people may enjoy content to a certain extent with AI, and such a market will exist. Disney tried to monetize with AI players. At Sony, especially when it comes to AI, is it a possibility that you try to monetize by tying up with AI players? What is your position currently? That's my first question. Second, about the mobility business: even if this mobility business is discontinued or downsized, I think there might be future opportunities. How can you leverage the Sony Mobility experience going forward?

**Q7 — Junya Ayada**
This is Ayada from JPMorgan Securities. I also have two questions. First, about game and network services: what is the long-term growth upside? MAU seems to be struggling to grow and first-party results fluctuate year-by-year. If profitability is to increase long term, what factors support that? Do you think MAU will still grow in emerging countries? As you said in the presentation, if platform engagement goes up with AI, maybe that would increase ARPU. Or are there other levers in the first-party game area? Please tell me about the upside of gaming. Second, about catalog investment in the music business: compared to other majors, you have been very active investing in catalogs. In the catalog investment market, AI could create AI-generated music. What is the impact on catalog valuations? Would valuations go up or down? Based on this, you also said you will be actively investing in catalogs going forward. Does the spread of AI affect that stance?

**Q8 — Ryosuke Katsura**
This is Katsura from SMBC Nikko Securities. I have two questions about capital allocation and the TSMC joint venture. First, regarding capital allocation and strategic investment in this mid-range plan: what level of strategic investment has been deployed so far? With multiples for entertainment companies going down, but you see opportunity, and your stock price has fallen drastically, you plan to increase capital allocation but also return to shareholders. How do you think about that balance? Second, for the I&SS and TSMC joint venture: the METI announcement mentioned an approximately JPY 80 billion investment subsidy. Given this national security and economic security context, how do you think about the JV with TSMC — its potential to increase share and improve profitability, and how will it affect mid- to long-term returns?

**Q9 — Yasuo Nakane**
Nakane from Mizuho Securities. One question. About activities to improve operations: for example, R&D last time was JPY 760 billion versus JPY 700 billion this fiscal year. What is the change and how are you trying to optimize R&D? Also, different business segments have issues: pictures ROIC is low and game development has changing demand. Operation optimization is being carried out but it's difficult to see from outside. Could you explain any improvements and typical examples?
