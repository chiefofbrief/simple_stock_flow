## 4. Risks and headwinds flagged by management

> Even though costs to streamline risk assets and strengthen the business foundation increased, business performance remained solid.

> The market conditions in Q4 FY25 trended essentially in line with our February forecast despite geopolitical risk in various regions and concerns about a macroeconomic slowdown.

> G&NS: （－）Recording of impairment losses against Bungie, Inc.'s intangible and other assets (Q2: 31.5 bln yen, Q4: 88.6 bln yen, FY25 total: 120.1 bln yen)

> ET&S: （－）Decrease in unit sales in Displays; We expect to contain at approximately 30 billion yen the impact of the increase in memory prices on our FY26 forecast through procurement, design and sales actions in various regions.

> I&SS: Because of our cautious view of the growth of the sensor market, due to our view that the trend toward larger-sized sensors for smartphones will moderate and the uncertainty regarding the impact of memory market conditions will remain, we have incorporated into our FY26 forecast a slight year-on-year decrease in the overall sales of mobile sensors. In Q4 FY25, the impact of memory market conditions gradually became more apparent in the smartphone market, especially in the low-end.

> All Other: （－）Recording of an additional share of loss of investments accounted for using the equity method in All Other, resulting from the discontinuation of the launch of Sony Honda Mobility's electric vehicle models (44.9 bln yen). * The additional losses have been recorded in FY2025 and are expected to be recorded in FY2026. These losses are already incorporated in the FY2026 operating income forecast.

> Financial income and expenses: -91.7 bln yen（－）Decrease in unrealized gains mainly on Sony's shares of Spotify Technology S.A.

## 5. Critical Accounting Estimates

Not found in filing. Sony files on Form 20-F under IFRS Accounting Standards. The source document provided is the earnings results release presentation, not the full 20-F. Critical accounting estimates (goodwill impairment, content asset amortization, revenue recognition, etc.) would be in the Form 20-F annual report. The financial results release discloses that the Financial Services business has been classified as a discontinued operation under IFRS from Q1 FY25, following the Board's resolution on May 14, 2025 to execute the partial spin-off (completed October 1, 2025). From Q3 FY25, Sony applies the equity method to the Financial Services business (SFGI shares retained), recording profit/loss as operating income/loss in continuing operations.
