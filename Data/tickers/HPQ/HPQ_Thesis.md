#### Section 6: Preliminary Hypothesis

**Q19. Preliminary hypothesis**

**Numbers (anticipated):**

The narrative is in a precarious state: formed around AI PC momentum but not validated by earnings improvement.

The key path to price realization: the AI PC cycle generates meaningful ASP expansion and eventually unit volume recovery; HP IQ/Device-as-a-Service drives recurring revenue beyond one-time hardware refresh; memory cost normalization in FY2027 restores EPS to or above prior levels. This is a 12–24 month story, not 3–6 months.

**Scenario:**
Current price ($26.24, GAAP P/E 9.58x, non-GAAP P/E 7.83x) appears to embed a base-to-bull case for the AI PC cycle: successful pass-through of memory costs to customers, durable demand from Windows 11 + AI PC adoption, and eventual EPS recovery toward $3.00+ as commodity costs normalize. The LOSER opportunity (buying below fair value when price was down 31%) has largely closed — the stock has re-rated. What remains at $26+ is not a discounted business but rather one priced at approximately fair value on the AI PC base case, with meaningful downside if H2 2026 earnings disappoint. The current price embeds a scenario that requires H2 EPS execution near guidance despite known headwinds — not an extraordinary outcome, but also not one offering a margin of safety given the elevated risk.

**Thesis:**
HPQ was a classic LOSER opportunity in the $17–19 range (January–April 2026): a GAAP-profitable, dividend-paying business with stable earnings beaten down by temporary cost headwinds and CEO departure. The stock has since re-rated +40% on AI PC momentum (NVIDIA RTX Spark partnership). At $26.24, the LOSER margin of safety is gone — the stock trades above all analyst targets, with analysts collectively maintaining bearish/neutral stances and a median target implying -25.7% downside. The question has shifted from "is this too cheap?" to "has the rally overshot?"

Evidence that would confirm the thesis survives (downside bounded): H2 FY2026 non-GAAP EPS at or above $0.66 guidance midpoint × 2 = $1.32 H2; Q3 earnings confirming no further guidance cuts; FY2027 non-GAAP EPS guidance at or above $3.00 (i.e., no further multi-year earnings compression).
