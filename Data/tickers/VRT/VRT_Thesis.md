**Reflexivity assessment:** This is a meaningful reflexivity signal. (1) VRT's rising stock price has enabled investment-grade upgrades and a $4.6B financing overhaul at favorable rates — lower cost of capital directly enables the $425-525M 2026 capex plan that funds revenue growth. (2) S&P 500 inclusion creates forced passive buying that feeds price appreciation independent of fundamentals. (3) Strong stock price enables M&A (BMarko, Strategic Thermal Labs, ThermoKey) that strengthens the competitive position. The self-reinforcing mechanism is present but not yet dominant — fundamentals are still driving the primary story. [INFERRED: GEMINI.md reflexivity framework applied to VRT's stock-price-to-financing loop]

Direction: Enthusiasm is running ahead of demonstrated results on an absolute valuation basis, but the fundamental improvement trajectory is genuine and accelerating. Both conditions are present simultaneously.
