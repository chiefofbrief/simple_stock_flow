# Earnings Call Q&A: CRM

**Quarters Analyzed:** 2027Q1, 2026Q4

---
# CURRENT QUARTER: 2027Q1

**Brent Thill** (Analyst, Jefferies)
Curious to get your thoughts on just the transformation to an AI led story — Agentforce numbers are great, but what else are you most excited about, what signals from customer pipeline, any other metrics you can share?

**Marc Benioff** (Chair and CEO)
We are using it ourselves more than ever — four million autonomous service transactions on help.salesforce.com and 1-800-NOSOFTWARE. We qualified 220,000 leads autonomously in Q1 generating $42 million in pipeline. The biggest technology moment in the quarter: Agentforce replaces Salesforce search — available in every app, in Slack, in Microsoft Teams, in a Salesforce Coworker phone app. The rate of innovation far exceeds customer adoption speed right now; bringing customers in to model use cases is mission critical.

**Keith** (Analyst)
Where do you garner confidence in back-half organic subscription revenue acceleration? CRPO has been spot in line with guidance for two quarters — not outperforming. Tableau dragging, Commerce Cloud dragging. Strong Agentforce KPIs but bookings not coming through. Help put both sides together.

**Robin Washington** (Chief Operating and Finance Officer)
CRPO is a leading indicator but renewal timing matters increasingly with consumption. We raised overall FY27 guidance. The two metrics we track going back to October: net new AOV acceleration greater than AOV — we saw that in H2 FY26 and have high confidence for H1 FY27. That leads to revenue reacceleration in H2. Pipeline very strong. Over 50% of Agentforce/Data 360 bookings from existing customers. We're expanding TAM.

**Miguel Milano** (President and Chief Revenue Officer)
Confident on subscription and support organic reacceleration in constant currency in H2. Informatica was growing single-digit on bookings and revenue; in two quarters we significantly reaccelerated it — now in double-digit revenue growth. 98 deals above $1M in net new AOV. Top 10 deals: combined annual incremental booking grew 60% year-over-year; TCV of those 10 deals added approximately $800 million — 2.5x the same 10 deals last year. Seven of top 10 deals added seats. Three monetization levers: (1) upgrading existing seats to unlimited AI access (A1E up 60%); (2) new seat pockets unlocked by transformed clouds; (3) customer-facing use cases via flex credits — six of top 10 deals were ELAs with flex credits. Fourth lever: Headless 360 bringing platform to surfaces never previously monetized.

**Gabrielle** (Analyst)
Headless strategy — how does it intersect with the build vs. buy debate? How do you protect the downside from customers potentially building in-house or enabling value abstraction out of Salesforce?

**Patrick Stokes** (President and Chief Marketing Officer)
Headless makes it easier to implement Salesforce — coding agents (Claude, Codex) use MCP to connect to Salesforce. Agentforce customers in production grew 50% this quarter — partly from this. Customers are not using coding agents to rebuild Salesforce; they're using them to get more value out of Salesforce in different ways. Salesforce is the most used set of APIs on the planet (Postman data). MCP is the new layer that makes this work for the agent-building era.

**Srinivas Tallapragada** (President and Chief Engineering Officer)
Since launch less than a month ago: Headless MCP tool calls more than 1.5 million; Slack MCP tool calls 50 million. Latent demand for Salesforce in the flow of work — people need trusted infrastructure (compliance, security, permissioning) that they can access from any surface. New monetization area — working with customers and partners on fair value capture.

**Miguel Milano**
Two concrete examples: Echo — existing customer, moved into Data Cloud and Agentforce, now using Headless to let agents built on AI labs access Salesforce data. Anthropic — uses Sales Cloud from Headless perspective via Coworker and other apps; Slack usage exploded fivefold through Q1.

**Brad Zelnick** (Analyst, Deutsche Bank)
AWU and token consumption are enormous and growing fast. Can you translate usage metrics to revenue? Agentforce ARR is impressive but usage suggests faster adoption. Also: gross margins show no degradation despite surging token demand — how?

**Marc Benioff** (Chair and CEO)
Engineering headcount has been flat (~15,000) for two years — AI tools creating efficiency so we don't need more. Headcount growth concentrated in sales (Miguel's area). Margin story is: not hiring more engineers, not hiring more G&A. Token usage: we are spending significantly with OpenAI (Codex) and Anthropic and absorbing that into our margin structure. We're absorbing it into cost of revenues without degrading margins. We delivered 28.6 trillion tokens — may be among the first companies reporting at that level of detail.

**Robin Washington** (Chief Operating and Finance Officer)
On monetization: AWU measures work done with customers and internally. Top 10 AWU customers have spent more than 1.5x over the past year. Monetization occurring over time via consumption and getting more value from core platforms. On FY30 Rule of 50: customer zero (running our own business on Salesforce) is key to how we will improve productivity and reach the framework — grow top line to $63B+ while expanding non-GAAP margins.

**Kirk Materne** (Analyst, Evercore)
Slack as a gateway for broader agentic adoption — what you're seeing now and how it stacks up in pipeline, given it seems like an unbelievable network effect product?

**Miguel Milano** (President and Chief Revenue Officer)
Slack bookings booming, net new AOV very impressive, very little attrition. Slack MCP server tool calls mostly from builders building applications needing Slack's rich context. Three million custom apps built on Slack in Q1 — eight times quarter over quarter. Of those, 250,000 were AI agents — more than doubled quarter over quarter, eightfold year over year. Slack nearly half of million-plus wins this quarter, up 80% year-over-year. Agentforce/Slack partnership with Anthropic for Slackbot driving ~3% company productivity improvement.

**Srinivas Tallapragada** (President and Chief Engineering Officer)
Slack is where humans and agents work together visibly — agents listen on channels, respond to requests, do PR reviews. Developer community sees this manifestation most. As it expands to general knowledge workers, Slack will become even more prominent.

**Marc Benioff** (Chair and CEO)
Sales is a $10B cloud. Service is a $10B cloud. Data is already a $10B cloud. With Slack's current growth rate (ACV incredible in Q1, AWUs up 350% quarter over quarter), Slack will be a $10B cloud in short order — started at less than $1B in revenue when acquired, was struggling. The coupling with Salesforce distribution plus Agentforce embedding is the moment Slack is having.

# PRIOR QUARTER: 2026Q4

**Operator** (Operator)
And your first question will come from Keith Weiss with Morgan Stanley.

**Keith Weiss** (Analyst, Morgan Stanley)
Congratulations on a solid end to FY '26, especially with the numbers associated with Agentforce. However, CRPO growth was somewhat disappointing, growing at 9%. Can you talk about the ability of Salesforce to grow the Agentforce business while also bringing along the entire portfolio of products?

**Marc Benioff** (Chair and CEO)
It's a great question. Salesforce is a comprehensive business. We’ve been innovating, and are integrating our legacy business to support future growth. We are grateful for what we've been able to achieve.

**Robin Washington** (CFO)
We're monetizing AI in multiple ways and are seeing great growth in our premium SKUs. Our seats are continuing to grow. We're excited about the incremental value that Agentforce brings, which was illuminated through our customer successes shared today.

**Brent Thill** (Analyst, Jefferies)
With the $50 billion buyback announced, why not lean harder into M&A versus buying back stock?

**Marc Benioff** (Chair and CEO)
There are many uses of cash such as dividends, buybacks, and acquisitions. Our board approved a 5.8% increase in our quarterly dividend. Our approach focuses on using cash effectively and keeping our balance sheet strong.

**Robin Washington** (CFO)
Acquisitions haven't been set aside in our strategy. We're still looking at ways to grow organically and via acquisitions as well.

**Kirk Materne** (Analyst, Evercore)
What is your perspective on the potential for AI model providers to become competitors? Can you clarify your strategy moving forward?

**Marc Benioff** (Chair and CEO)
The strategic question is whether these models become platforms. Our job as a software company is to help customers find success by connecting with them in innovative ways.

**Gabriela Borges** (Analyst, Goldman Sachs)
Can you explain the $2.4 billion in AWUs? How do you monetize this, and what impact does it have on gross margin?

**Robin Washington** (CFO)
In the short run, we anticipate being neutral on gross margins. We’re building efficiencies into our product development to control costs and drive margins higher over time.

**Patrick Stokes** (President and Chief Marketing Officer)
We've started measuring Agentic Work Units as actual work being done through agents. This helps illustrate our customers' ROI from using our platform.

**Miguel Milano** (President and Chief Revenue Officer)
We're seeing significant growth in net new AOV, which I believe will lead to accelerated revenue growth in the second half. We've also successfully added AI elements to our products.

**Michael Spencer** (Executive Vice President of Finance and Investor Relations)
