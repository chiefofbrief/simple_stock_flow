**Mike Spencer** (EVP Finance)

**Marc Benioff** (Chair and CEO)
This was really an outstanding quarter for Salesforce. We have delivered record revenue, record deals, and just incredible cashflow. And we've also returned record levels to our investors. We've processed 28.6 trillion tokens, up 152% quarter over quarter — 3.8 billion agentic work units for our customers, up 111% quarter-over-quarter. Agentforce ARR is now greater than a billion dollars. Combined with Data 360 and Informatica Cloud, we've delivered 3.4 billion in AI and data ARR. 50% of Agentforce and Data 360 bookings were from existing customers expanding their commitment. We secured a record 98 Q1 deals with over $1 million in new ACV in the quarter. Q1 revenue was $11.13 billion, up 13% year-over-year nominal and 12% in constant currency. cRPO, $33.6 billion, up approximately 14% nominal and 13% in constant currency. Non-GAAP operating margin of 34.8%, up 250 basis points. GAAP operating margin of 21.1%, up 130 basis points. $6.7 billion in operating cashflow.

Agentforce is now the search bar for every Salesforce application — one billion searches per month. Agentforce Coworker now embedded across every app, no configuration required. Headless 360 announced at TDX in March — making the entire platform accessible via MCP to any coding agent or surface. Since April launch: 4.5 million MCP calls into the platform; nearly a trillion API calls in Q1 alone. Slack was nearly half of million-plus wins this quarter, up 80% year-over-year. Slack AWUs grew nearly 350% quarter-over-quarter. One million users of Slack MCP in first six weeks.

**Robin Washington** (Chief Operating and Finance Officer)
Sales, service and Slack collectively represented more than 60% of Q1 net new AOV. Bookings for A1E and A4X (premium SKUs in sales and service) grew nearly 60% year-over-year. Our top 10 customers by Q1 AWU usage have increased their total Salesforce spend by 1.5x in the last year. Q1 revenue outperformed guide — outperformance driven by Informatica on-prem business and professional services timing. CRPO partially offset by softness in commerce and Tableau. AI coding tools enabled us to double features and code shipped year-over-year while reducing incidents and defects. Slackbot driving 3.8 million hours of annualized productivity gains for employees.

Guidance update: We are raising the midpoint of our FY27 revenue guidance to $45.9 billion to $46.2 billion; continue to expect subscription and support growth of approximately 11% year-over-year in constant currency. Reiterating non-GAAP operating margin guidance of 34.3%. Adjusting GAAP operating margin guidance to 20.6%, driven by higher restructuring. Operating cashflow and free cash flow growth updated to 4% to 5% year-over-year (approximately 5-point headwind from debt tied to ASR). Q2 revenue guidance: $11.27 billion to $11.35 billion, approximately 10% constant currency growth. Q2 CRPO growth: approximately 13% year-over-year in constant currency. FY30 revenue target: $63 billion+ (Rule of 50 framework, targeting non-GAAP operating margin expansion alongside revenue growth).

First half net new AOV growth expected to outpace AOV growth and drive organic revenue reacceleration in second half of FY27. Q2 and FY27 guidance reflect continued momentum in Agentforce, Data 360 and Slack, partially offset by ongoing weakness in marketing and commerce, and increased softness in Tableau bookings and renewals. Greater license revenue volatility expected with Informatica on-prem revenue addition.
