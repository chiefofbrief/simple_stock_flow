# Earnings Call — Analyst Questions: CRM

**Quarters:** 2027Q1, 2026Q4

*Analyst questions only — management responses are in CRM_earnings_qa.md*

## CURRENT QUARTER: 2027Q1

**Q1 — Brent Thill (Jefferies)**
Curious to get your thoughts on the transformation to an AI-led story — Agentforce numbers are great, but what else are you most excited about, what signals from the customer pipeline and any other metrics you can share?

**Q2 — Keith (analyst, firm not specified)**
Where do you garner confidence in back-half organic subscription revenue acceleration? CRPO has been spot in line with guidance for two quarters, not outperforming. Tableau and Commerce Cloud are dragging. Strong Agentforce KPIs but bookings not coming through — can you help put those two sides of the debate together?

**Q3 — Gabrielle (analyst, firm not specified)**
On the Headless strategy and specifically how it intersects with the build vs. buy debate — how do you protect your downside from customers potentially building in-house or from competitors using Headless to abstract value out of Salesforce? What is your monetization strategy?

**Q4 — Brad Zelnick (Deutsche Bank)**
AWU and token consumption metrics are some of the biggest and fastest growing in software and seem to validate customer usage. Can you help translate the usage metrics to revenue? And separately, gross margins show no degradation despite surging token demand — how are you doing that?

**Q5 — Kirk Materne (Evercore)**
Can you talk about Slack as a potential gateway for broader agentic adoption — what you're seeing now, how it's stacking up in pipeline, and whether it's having an impact on broader agentic bookings as you go into the back half?
