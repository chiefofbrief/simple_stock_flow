#### Section 6: Preliminary Hypothesis

**Q19. Preliminary hypothesis.**

**Numbers:**
The financial statements are expected to show: (1) a genuine high-quality operating business generating OCF scaling at ~34% CAGR alongside revenue; (2) GAAP losses that are almost entirely attributable to non-cash SBC from the Liquidity Event Share Plan and IFRS 16 lease interest — not to operating deterioration; (3) unit economics that remain intact at the store level based on gross margin trends (+20bps in Q1 2026); (4) negative working capital as a structural feature (grocer float model), not a red flag; (5) ROIC turned negative in FY2025 (-5.9% annual, -7.8% TTM) — this needs investigation. The ROIC deterioration may reflect the combined drag of SBC losses on net income and expanding invested capital from the store rollout. Pass 1 must verify whether ROIC at the store level (ex-SBC distortion) is intact.

**Narrative & Catalyst:**
The narrative is institutionally-driven and well-formed. There is no meaningful retail sentiment to track (social data absent). The BIM-of-Mexico thesis has traction with quality institutional investors (Druckenmiller, JP Morgan Overweight, HSBC Buy). The near-term catalyst window (Q2 earnings + lockup clearing) is immediate — this analysis is being conducted on the cusp of the Q2 report. The path to price realization is primarily earnings trajectory + continued store growth demonstrating the model's scalability beyond Central Mexico.

**Scenario:**
The current price of $42.24 and market cap of ~$4.9B USD implies approximately 1.04x TTM revenue (~$4.7B USD equivalent) and ~15.9x TTM OCF. For a company compounding revenue at 33%+ and OCF at 34%+, these multiples are not extreme — they embed continued strong growth at roughly the current pace, but do not require an extraordinary acceleration. The embedded scenario is roughly: **base case** — not bull case. The stock does not appear to price in hyper-acceleration; it prices in continuation of the demonstrated growth trajectory. The primary embedded assumption is that the SBC charges are transitory and GAAP profitability surfaces within 2–3 years as the vesting schedule runs off. A downside scenario where SSS decelerates materially (e.g., to <10%) or where geographic expansion outside Central Mexico disappoints would challenge the current valuation.

**Thesis:**
TBBB is a high-quality, high-velocity growth retailer executing a hard discount model in Mexico's large and underpenetrated grocery market. OCF generation is real and scaling with revenue. GAAP losses are almost entirely non-cash (SBC) or IFRS-driven (lease capitalization) — not operating deterioration. The stock is near all-time highs with institutional backing and a compelling long-term compounder thesis. The risk is that the stock prices in a continuation of 33%+ growth while leaving little room for execution missteps — particularly the FRESH initiative (fresh food expansion is higher capital intensity), new region performance outside Central Mexico (mixed signals in Q&A), and the ERP implementation risk. At ~1x revenue and ~16x OCF, there is some margin of safety in the multiple; the margin of safety in the business comes from the demonstrated track record of execution, not from a deep discount to intrinsic value.

**Evidence that would confirm:** Q2 2026 showing continued SSS growth >12%, OCF growth tracking or accelerating vs. revenue, FRESH trial showing positive initial unit economics, new region stores ramping to expected levels.

**Evidence that would break it:** SSS deceleration to <10% in mature regions suggesting saturation, FCF turning structurally negative as CapEx accelerates without OCF to match, ROIC at the store level deteriorating (not just aggregate distorted by SBC), major execution issues with ERP or regional expansion.

**Q20. Pass 1 focus questions:**
1. What is the true store-level ROIC excluding SBC and IFRS 16 distortions? Does it confirm the unit economics management presents (~MXN30M annual revenue per store by year 3)?
2. Is OCF/FCF generation tracking the revenue trajectory — or is the capex ramp widening the FCF gap to a concerning degree?
3. What is the SBC trajectory? Is the Liquidity Event Share Plan a one-time event or will it continue at similar scale through the vesting schedule?
4. How does the balance sheet look post-follow-on offering? What is the cash position and debt structure?
5. What does the ROIC breakdown reveal — is the TTM negative ROIC a timing artifact of expansion investments or a sign that invested capital is outrunning returns?
6. Does the working capital picture (deeply negative) reflect genuine supplier financing (a structural advantage) or a liability that could reverse under stress?
7. What does the FCF/capex ratio imply for the company's ability to self-fund continued 600-store-per-year expansion, and at what point does it need to return to capital markets?

**Revenue**

TBBB's revenue growth is exceptional and the quality of that growth is high. The 5-year CAGR is 33.1% per FMP (35.6% per the 20-F, 2022–2025), with FY2025 revenue reaching Ps.78.2B (US$4.4B) [CONFIRMED: TBBB_mda_excerpts.md, 20-F management statement]. The annual growth rate has decelerated modestly: 38.8% (2023) → 30.3% (2024) → 27.6% (2025 per FMP); TTM growth is driven by Q1 2026's +33.5% YoY [CONFIRMED: TBBB_financial_analysis.md; TBBB_mda_excerpts.md, 6-K]. The deceleration from 2022–2023 highs is expected as the absolute revenue base grows — but the *composition* of Q1 2026 growth is distinctively high-quality: 18.0% transaction growth + 13.6% avg ticket increase (volume and basket depth, not price) + 16.0% same-store sales [CONFIRMED: TBBB_mda_excerpts.md, 6-K]. Revenue quality check: DSO is essentially zero — "most of our sales from merchandise are received in cash at the time of sale" [CONFIRMED: TBBB_mda_6k.md, line 208]. No receivables accumulation, no channel-stuffing risk. Quarterly sequential momentum: Ps.18.77B (Q2 2025) → Ps.20.04B (Q3) → Ps.21.62B (Q4) → Ps.22.86B (Q1 2026) — the QoQ increments (+9.6%, +6.7%, +7.9%, +5.7%) are naturally moderating as the base grows, but the absolute dollar increases remain substantial. Versus PSMT: TBBB's 33.1% CAGR dwarfs PSMT's 9.8% (CV 0.15), and TBBB's revenue base (Ps.83B TTM) is now ~14.6x PSMT's ($5.7B TTM) despite starting far smaller. Revenue CV of 0.43 (vs. PSMT 0.15) reflects growth, not instability — the trend is persistently upward with no disruption.

*TL;DR: Revenue growth is exceptional in rate, duration, and quality — volume-driven, zero receivable risk, with 580+ net new stores per year as the primary engine. The relevant risk is deceleration, not reversal.*

**Operating Margin**

The GAAP operating margin — -0.7% (FY2025, per financial_analysis.md) and -1.0% (TTM) — is entirely misleading as a profitability signal. The distortion is quantifiable: FY2025 SBC of Ps.2,930,222 (including Ps.1,953,194 from the Liquidity Event Share Plan alone) [CONFIRMED: TBBB_notes.md, line 2002–2003] converted a business generating ~5.6% Adjusted EBITDA margin into one reporting an operating loss. Adjusted EBITDA from management's own reconciliation: Ps.4,384,287 (FY2025) at 5.6% margin; Ps.1,275,704 (Q1 2026) at 5.6% margin [CONFIRMED: TBBB_mda_6k.md, lines 335, 341]. The five-year adj EBITDA margin trajectory: 5.1% (FY2023) → 5.9% (FY2024) → 5.6% (FY2025) → 5.6% (Q1 2026) [CONFIRMED: TBBB_mda_6k.md, lines 383]. This is not deteriorating — it is remarkably stable and shows slight improvement. The Q3 2025 quarterly operating margin of -4.4% was an artifact of a single large SBC recognition event (Ps.1,573,926 in Q3 2025 vs. Ps.213,290 in Q1 2025) [CONFIRMED: TBBB_financial_analysis.md, quarterly SBC]. Q1 2026 at -0.1% operating margin with Ps.721,505 in SBC — subtract SBC and Q1 2026 operating margin is approximately +2.1% on a cash basis [ESTIMATED: Q1 2026 operating loss Ps.18,430 + SBC Ps.721,505 / revenue Ps.22,860,346 = 3.1% adj operating margin]. Versus PSMT: PSMT's 4.4% operating margin (TTM, stable since 2021, CV 0.03) is the clean benchmark — a mature discount retailer's normalized margin. TBBB's adj EBITDA of 5.6% is not far from PSMT's EBITDA equivalent (~6.1%, adding PSMT's D&A/Revenue of 1.7%) despite being in aggressive expansion mode. The path from 5.6% to higher adj EBITDA (7-10% at maturity per the BIM thesis) runs through: G&A operating leverage (HQ costs should grow below revenue going forward per Q4 2025 management guidance), higher private label penetration beyond 58%, and FRESH category margin expansion.

*TL;DR: GAAP operating margin is uninformative due to SBC; underlying adjusted profitability is 5.6% and stable, approaching mature peer levels while still in aggressive expansion. The margin story is not broken.*

**Operating Cash Flow**

OCF is the primary quality signal in this business, and it is genuinely exceptional. OCF has compounded at 34.4% CAGR (2021–2025), precisely tracking revenue growth — this is the hallmark of high-quality cash conversion [CONFIRMED: TBBB_financial_analysis.md, 5yr CAGR]. TTM OCF: Ps.5,480,656 (+19.0% on FY2025 of Ps.4,461,339) [CONFIRMED: TBBB_financial_analysis.md]. Quarterly trajectory: Ps.0.76B (Q2 2025) → Ps.1.14B (Q3) → Ps.1.62B (Q4) → Ps.1.96B (Q1 2026) — clear and consistent acceleration [CONFIRMED: TBBB_financial_analysis.md, quarterly]. Q1 2026 OCF of Ps.1,960,664 represents +64.1% YoY vs. Q1 2025 of Ps.1,194,848 [CONFIRMED: TBBB_financial_metrics.json, quarterly OCF]. This acceleration materially exceeds the 33.5% revenue growth rate, implying improving cash conversion efficiency. The driver: negative working capital growing more negative (Ps.-3.05B Q1 2025 → Ps.-6.37B Q1 2026), which reflects supplier terms extended as the store count grows. This is structurally positive — TBBB effectively runs on supplier financing, and the OCF benefit of growing negative WC is real and recurring. Versus PSMT: PSMT's OCF ($0.27B TTM, 5yr CAGR 19.7%) is dramatically smaller in absolute terms and grows at half TBBB's rate. TBBB generates 20x more absolute OCF despite revenue that is "only" 14.6x larger — this reflects the structural cash conversion advantage of TBBB's hard-discount model vs. PSMT's capital-intensive warehouse club format. OCF/revenue TTM = 5,480,656 / 83,285,609 = 6.6% — this is the true cash margin of the business.

*TL;DR: OCF is real, growing at the same rate as revenue, and accelerating in 2026. The business generates genuine cash — the SBC-driven GAAP losses do not change this reality. 6.6% OCF margin is the actual economic performance.*

**Free Cash Flow**

FCF is the one metric that reveals real financial tension in this model. FCF 5-year CAGR is 8.0% — dramatically below the 34.4% OCF CAGR — because capital expenditures are growing faster than both revenue and OCF [CONFIRMED: TBBB_financial_analysis.md].
