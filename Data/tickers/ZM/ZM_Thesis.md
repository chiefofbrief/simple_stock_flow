**Part B — Synthesis**

**1. What do revenue growth and operating margins reveal about the health and durability of the core business?**

Revenue growth of 3-5% masks a two-speed business: Enterprise (60% of revenue, growing 6-7%) is the durable, expanding franchise; Online (40%, growing 1-3%) is the declining-consumer segment that has stabilized at record-low churn (2.8%) [CONFIRMED: ZM_earnings_remarks.md]. The mix shift toward Enterprise is value-accretive because Enterprise customers have higher ARPC, better retention, and are the primary vehicle for Contact Center and Phone cross-sell. Operating margins improving from 5.6% to 23.1% over 3 years while revenue grew only $0.5B demonstrates that the entire margin improvement is driven by SBC normalization and operational efficiency — not revenue leverage. This is simultaneously good (real structural improvement) and concerning (the easiest portion of margin expansion is behind them; further improvement requires revenue acceleration or continued SBC cuts). The business is durable: it has operating leverage in the fixed-cost base, improving retention metrics, and a growing installed base of enterprise customers with expanding products.

**2. Do cash flow metrics confirm or contradict what the income statement shows?**
