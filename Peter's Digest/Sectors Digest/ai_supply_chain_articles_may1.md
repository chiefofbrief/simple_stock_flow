Constraints and Bottlenecks (Demand > Supply)
Physical Interconnect Limits: Traditional copper interconnects have reached their physical limits due to the massive data movement demands of scaling AI clusters.
Alignment Precision: A severe physical bottleneck occurs during optical coupling. A single-mode fiber core has a cross-sectional area of approximately 78.5 μm², while an optical waveguide is only about 0.099 μm²—a difference of nearly 800 times. Without nanometer-level alignment precision, coupling loss is enormous.

PIC Components: Unlike traditional EICs, PICs contain multiple optical components, including couplers, modulators, photodetectors (PD), optical filters, and optical waveguides.
Advanced Metrology Platforms: Companies like Enlitech have launched add-on hyperspectral imaging analysis systems (like the Night Jar platform) that can pinpoint light leakage and measure quantitative insertion loss (IL) values for specific waveguide segments.
The Four Critical Testing Stages
Testing an Optical Engine requires expertise across electrical, optical, and optoelectronic interactions. The testing pipeline involves four distinct stages:
PIC Wafer-Level Test (OWAT): The most critical stage in the process. Adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, or Ebitda, were $896 million. Earnings per share included a $4 billion gain related to the acquisition of Prolec GE.
Orders were $18.3 billion, almost double sales and up 71% year over year.
Wall Street was looking for earnings per share of $1.97 from sales of $9.2 billion. Ebitda was expected to be $770 million.
A year ago, GE Vernova reported earnings per share of $1.07 from sales of $8 billion, according to FactSet. Orders were $10.2 billion. Ebitda was $457 million.
The move is impressive considering that coming into Wednesday trading, GE Vernova stock was already up 204% over the past 12 months. Business has just been that good. The company expects to reach a backlog of $200 billion by the end of 2026, two years faster than it had previously expected.
Vernova has about 100 gigawatts of power turbines under contract. It shipped four gigawatts in the quarter. AI data centers account for about 20%, and traditional customers, including utilities, account for the rest.
