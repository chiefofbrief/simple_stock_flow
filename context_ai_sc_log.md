# AI Supply Chain — Company Log

*Last updated: —*

Per-company synthesis log for the AI supply chain coverage universe. Each entry draws from the company's thesis file and verbatim report excerpts to answer six questions: SC role, demand and capital flows, monetization and returns, narrative and reflexivity, competition, and supply chain implication.

**How this file is used:**
- Cross-company pattern recognition: where resources are moving, what's contracted vs. speculative, where reflexivity is running ahead of fundamentals
- Bottleneck mapping: which layers and companies are gating the AI buildout
- Implication synthesis: what each company's data tells us about the supply chain state as a whole

**Entry sources:** Thesis files (`Data/tickers/{TICKER}/{TICKER}_Thesis.md`) + verbatim report excerpts extracted via `prompt_sc_report_extract.md`.
