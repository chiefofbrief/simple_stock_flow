## Current State

### Macro & Rates
[Current direction of Treasury yields, Fed expectations, and key economic data trend.]
Treasury yields have ticked slightly down (10-Year at 4.26%, 2-Year at 3.76%) as markets focus on hopes for Middle East peace talks and a strong impending earnings season, attempting to move on from Iran War fears. However, inflation pressure remains acute with March CPI at +0.87% MoM and Unemployment at 4.3%. A prevailing retail narrative is now highlighting severe valuation concerns, citing the Buffett Indicator hitting an all-time high of 232.6%. Resilient corporate earnings are currently providing a "cushion," though economists warn of a rough start to the spring housing season (existing home sales -3.6% in March).

### Commodities
[Current direction and prevailing narrative for oil, gold, and other key commodities.]
A profound divergence has emerged in the oil market. While Brent futures sit near $95.06, the physical market is signaling an active supply emergency; North Sea Forties barrels for immediate delivery reached a record $148.87—a massive premium over paper curves. The Strait of Hormuz remains closed, disrupting 20 million barrels per day (the largest disruption in history per the IEA), which the record 400 million barrel SPR release in March has failed to offset. French officials have explicitly warned that if the closure "lasts months, we're all in trouble."

### Market Sentiment
[Broad risk-on/risk-off posture and prevailing mood.]
Deceptively resilient; the S&P 500 (SPY) has returned to pre-Iran war levels (+2.83% MoM). Sentiment is currently split between a "buy the dip" narrative supported by major banks (JPM, MS) and a more cautious "liquidity exit" view that suggests markets are overly reliant on an optimistic, unverified ceasefire narrative while ignoring physical supply deficits. This cautious view is increasingly highlighting extreme valuation risk and irrational exuberance, perfectly illustrated by distressed shoe brand Allbirds spiking 400% after pivoting to "AI compute infrastructure."
