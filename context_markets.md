### Commodities
[Current direction and prevailing narrative for oil, gold, and other key commodities.]
A profound divergence has emerged in the oil market. While Brent futures sit near $95.06, the physical market is signaling an active supply emergency; North Sea Forties barrels for immediate delivery reached a record $148.87—a massive premium over paper curves. The Strait of Hormuz remains closed, disrupting 20 million barrels per day (the largest disruption in history per the IEA), which the record 400 million barrel SPR release in March has failed to offset. French officials have explicitly warned that if the closure "lasts months, we're all in trouble."
